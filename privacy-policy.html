About GuineaPal

GuineaPal helps guinea pig owners keep care information organised, including guinea pig profiles, weights, health notes, medication, vet records, daily care tasks, bonding notes, family tree details, and cloud backup.

Common Help Topics

  • Adding and editing guinea pig profiles
  • Tracking weights, health notes, medication, and vet records
  • Using the daily care checklist
  • Backing up and restoring data with cloud backup
  • Exporting reports or records

Important Note

GuineaPal is designed to help you stay organised, but it is not a replacement for veterinary advice. If you are worried about your guinea pig’s health, please contact a qualified vet.

Privacy

GuineaPal stores your care records for your own use. If you choose to use cloud backup, your app data can be restored on another device when you log in.
